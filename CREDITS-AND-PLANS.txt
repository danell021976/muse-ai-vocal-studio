MUSEAI — TEST PLANS AND CREDITS

Restart the server after installing: Ctrl+C, then node app.js. Refresh the browser.

Each Create requests two songs. Reserve 10 app credits total; charge 5 for each
song after its audio is saved. Confirmed failures return 5 credits per song.
Replicate charges are separate and are not refunded by this app ledger.

Free: 500 monthly credits and 10 monthly downloads.
Pro: 5,000 monthly credits and 25 monthly downloads.
Premium: 10,000 monthly credits and 60 monthly downloads.
All plan changes and extra-download purchases are TEST ONLY. No payment
details are collected and no money is charged. Prices remain undecided.
Extra-download bundles: 1, 10 or 20 downloads. Unused extras carry over.

Monthly allowances reset on the first day of the calendar month in UTC,
when the account is next accessed. Unused monthly credits/downloads expire.
Upgrades add only the difference from the highest credit allowance granted
that month. Existing download usage is retained. Downgrades take effect next
month. Existing users' saved remaining demo credits are imported once.
Spent credits are a lifetime total; monthly downloads used reset monthly.
Each separate Download click counts once; interrupted transfers can retry
with the same identifier without another deduction. Browser saving cannot
be proven by the server, so an authorized download consumes its allowance.

Download allowances apply to the app's Download button. Listening necessarily
delivers audio to the browser; these quotas are not DRM or copy protection.
Before public launch, add payment verification, signup abuse controls,
production hosting/security, and decide actual prices and renewal policies.

Recovery: requests have durable IDs and are not automatically resubmitted
when acceptance by Replicate is uncertain. Such jobs keep their credits
reserved and appear in Credit history. Known predictions can be checked or
saved again there. For an unknown prediction, the owner must review Replicate
history; stop the server and run node review-generation.js to list the jobs.
After reviewing a job, node review-generation.js release <job-id> releases its
app credits without contacting Replicate. This does not refund provider fees.

BACKUPS: stop the server, then copy the entire accounts and users folders to
another safe location. accounts/credits.sqlite contains balances, plans,
download entitlements and generation records; accounts/accounts.sqlite holds
logins. Keep them together with users and any SQLite -wal/-shm sidecars.
The library Backups button restores library content only; it deliberately
does not roll back credits, purchases or download usage.
